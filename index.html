Kaise kaam karta hai?

  1. Rules: 13 tarah ke common Indian scam tricks (OTP, KYC, digital arrest, bijli, parcel, task job, AnyDesk…) ke patterns banaye gaye hain.
  2. Match: JavaScript RegExp se message me ye patterns dhoondhe jaate hain.
  3. Score: har trick ka ek weight hai; sab jodkar 0–100 risk score banta hai.
  4. Samjhao: match hue shabd highlight hote hain aur har trick ki Hinglish explanation milti hai. "Suno" button se sun bhi sakte hain.
