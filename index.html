How to protect yourself

  • Use a privacy-focused browser (Firefox with strict tracking protection, or Brave).
  • Keep your browser updated and remove extensions you don't use.
  • Don't grant location, camera or notification permission to unknown sites.
  • Use a different browser for banking and for casual browsing.
