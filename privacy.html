Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1 · What data we collect

None. This site has no server, no database, no sign-up and no analytics. Everything you type or upload is processed by JavaScript inside your own browser, and it disappears when you close the page.

  • Scam Message Checker: the message you paste is checked on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • Post Se Pehle: the photo you choose is read on your device to show its hidden EXIF data. It is never uploaded. The "safe copy" is created on your device too.
  • Browser Jasoos: the device details it shows are read and displayed only to you. They are not stored or sent.
  • Pastejacking Demo: it writes a harmless sample text to your clipboard only when you press Copy. It never reads your clipboard.

2 · Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies, so it doesn't need a cookie banner. The only stored item is your light/dark theme choice in Antariksh, which is saved in your browser's local storage. It is strictly functional, stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

3 · Third parties and embeds

There are no ads, trackers, fonts, maps or videos loaded from other websites. All images, sounds and the video are original and served from this site. A few links open other sites only when you click them: OpenStreetMap (to show a photo's location), cybercrime.gov.in (the Government of India cyber-crime portal) and GitHub (source code). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, may keep basic technical server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security.

4 · Your rights and contact

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a privacy concern (for example under India's DPDP Act 2023 or the EU GDPR), please open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
