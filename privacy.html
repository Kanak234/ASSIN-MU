Privacy, Cookies & Terms

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What data we collect

None. The message you paste is checked only inside your own browser by JavaScript. It is never sent to any server, saved or shared.

Cookies & tracking

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no third-party embeds, so no cookie consent banner is needed. The only outside link points to the government site cybercrime.gov.in, and it opens only if you click it.

Terms of use

SandeshKavach is a free educational student project. It uses simple pattern rules and can make mistakes: a message marked safe can still be a scam, and a normal message can get flagged. Always verify through official sources. If you have been defrauded, call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in. No fees are charged, so no refund policy applies.
